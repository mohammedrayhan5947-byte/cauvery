Cauvery Resorts ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website (www.cauveryresorts.com) or contact us to make a booking enquiry.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you use our enquiry form, call us, or message us on WhatsApp, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Travel dates (check-in / check-out)
  • Number of guests and room preference
  • Email address (if provided voluntarily)

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, standard web server logs and browser cookies may collect:

  • Your IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
  • Referring website or search engine

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your booking enquiry via WhatsApp or phone
  • Confirm room availability and provide tariff details
  • Send booking confirmations and relevant stay information
  • Improve our website and user experience

We do not use your personal information for unsolicited marketing, spam, or third-party advertising.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. Your contact details are used only to respond to your enquiry and manage your stay at Cauvery Resorts.

We may share information only if required by law or by a competent government authority.

4. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies to improve your browsing experience. These include:

  • Session cookies — to remember your preferences within a single visit (e.g., cookie consent)
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use the site (no personally identifiable data is collected)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not prevent you from accessing any content on our site.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external services including Google Maps and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party platforms. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect information you share with us. WhatsApp messages and phone calls are handled privately by our team. We do not store booking enquiry data on public servers.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any future communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 2025  |  Cauvery Resorts, Near Bhagamandala, Coorg — 571247

By making a booking or enquiry with Cauvery Resorts, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before confirming your stay.

1. Bookings & Reservations

  • Bookings can be made via WhatsApp, phone call, or the enquiry form on our website.
  • A reservation is confirmed only after verbal or written confirmation from our team.
  • We recommend booking in advance, especially during peak pilgrimage seasons and festival weekends.
  • We reserve the right to decline a booking at our discretion.

2. Check-In & Check-Out

  • Standard check-in time: 12:00 PM
  • Standard check-out time: 11:00 AM
  • Early check-out from 5:00 AM is available on request for pilgrims (please inform us at the time of booking).
  • Late check-out may be available subject to availability and an additional charge.
  • Valid government-issued photo ID is required at check-in for all adult guests.

3. Payments & Tariff

  • Room tariffs are as listed on our Tariff page and are subject to change without prior notice.
  • Payments are accepted in cash at the property. Prior arrangement may be made for UPI/bank transfer for group bookings.
  • GST invoices are provided on request. Please share your GST number at the time of booking.
  • No advance payment is required to make an enquiry.

4. Cancellations

  • Cancellations must be informed via phone or WhatsApp at the earliest convenience.
  • We do not charge a cancellation fee, but we request reasonable notice so we can accommodate other guests.
  • For group bookings (10+ persons), please inform us at least 48 hours in advance in case of cancellation.

5. House Rules

All guests are expected to respect the sanctity of the premises. The following rules are strictly enforced:

  • No non-vegetarian food of any kind on the premises — including rooms, common areas, and parking.
  • No alcohol or tobacco anywhere on the property.
  • Guests must maintain cleanliness and respect for other guests, especially pilgrims.
  • Noise must be kept to a minimum between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.
  • Cauvery Resorts reserves the right to ask any guest to vacate the premises immediately for violation of house rules, without a refund.

6. Liability

  • Cauvery Resorts is not responsible for loss or damage to personal belongings of guests.
  • Guests are responsible for any damage caused to resort property during their stay.
  • We are not liable for events beyond our control, including natural calamities, power outages, or government-imposed restrictions.

7. Photography & Media

  • Guests are welcome to take photographs for personal use.
  • Commercial photography or filming on the premises requires prior written permission from management.

8. Website Use

  • All content on this website — including text, images, and branding — is the property of Cauvery Resorts and may not be copied or reproduced without permission.
  • We make every effort to keep information on the website accurate, but tariffs, availability, and details may change. Please confirm with us directly before travelling.

9. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka.

10. Changes to These Terms

We reserve the right to update these Terms & Conditions at any time. The most current version will always be available on this page.
