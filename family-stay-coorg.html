day1

Day 1 — Arrival & Bhagamandala

Check in at Cauvery Resorts. Visit Bhagamandala Triveni Sangam — take a holy dip and offer prayers at Bhagandeshwara Temple. Peaceful dinner at our pure veg restaurant.

Questions About
Family Stay in Coorg

Planning a family group of 10 or more with kids or grandparents? Call +91-944-9485133 and we'll tell you straight.

01Is Cauvery Resorts good for large families with elderly members?

Yes. We are specifically popular with multi-generational families. Our ground-floor rooms are easily accessible, the temple proximity means less travel fatigue for elderly members, and our sattvic, light vegetarian food is ideal for seniors.

02Are children welcome? Is there any extra charge?

Children are warmly welcome. Children below 5 years stay free. For children between 5–10 years, a nominal charge applies. Our staff is attentive and the property is completely safe for children to move around freely.

03What are the best family activities near Cauvery Resorts?

Talacauvery darshan, holy dip at Bhagamandala Triveni Sangam, Brahmagiri nature walk, Dubare elephant camp (45 km), Coorg coffee estate tours, Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat — something for every age group.

04How far is Coorg from Bangalore for a weekend family trip?

Bhagamandala (where Cauvery Resorts is located) is approximately 270 km from Bangalore — about 5–6 hours by road. It's a popular 2-night weekend trip for Bangalore family groups.

05Is there pure vegetarian food for the whole family?

Absolutely. Our restaurant is 100% pure vegetarian with South Indian breakfast, thali meals, and à la carte options. We cater to children's preferences and dietary needs — just let us know when booking.
