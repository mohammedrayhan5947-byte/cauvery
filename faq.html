Accommodation

What types of rooms are available at Cauvery Resorts?
We offer Standard Non-AC Rooms (₹1,500/night), Deluxe AC Rooms (₹2,500/night), and Pilgrim Dormitories (₹400/person) for large groups. All rooms are clean, well-maintained, and include attached bathrooms with hot water.
What are the check-in and check-out timings?
Standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. However, we offer an early 5:00 AM check-out for pilgrims who need to catch first darshan at Talacauvery or Bhagamandala. Please request this at the time of booking.
Is advance booking required or can we walk in?
Walk-in is welcome, but we strongly recommend advance booking — especially during peak pilgrimage seasons (Brahmotsava, Karthika month, festival weekends). Call or WhatsApp us to confirm availability before you travel.
Are monks and sanyasis welcome?
Absolutely. We warmly welcome monks, sanyasis, and spiritual practitioners. We offer room-only stays with no meal obligation and flexible, compassionate rates. Our premises are fully sattvic — no non-veg food, no alcohol, no tobacco.

Food & Restaurant

Is the food vegetarian? Are non-veg items available?
Our kitchen is 100% pure vegetarian. We serve only sattvic South Indian meals. Non-vegetarian food is not available and is strictly not permitted on our premises. This is a non-negotiable commitment we maintain for the sanctity of our environment.
What are the meal timings?
Breakfast: 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM. Lunch: 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM. Dinner: 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Evening snacks and tea are available from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Early breakfast is available for pilgrims on request.
Can we pre-book group meals?
Yes. We strongly recommend pre-booking meals for groups of 20 or more. We can also prepare packed prasad boxes for pilgrim processions. Please inform us at least 24 hours in advance with the number of people and meal preference.
Can we bring our own cook or chef for our pilgrim group?
Yes, absolutely. Pilgrim groups are welcome to bring their own cook or chef. We provide kitchen space, utensils, gas, and water. If your group does not have a cook, the resort owner will arrange one for you on request — just let us know your group size and requirements at the time of booking. All cooking must be 100% vegetarian (sattvic) as per our premises policy.
Is alcohol allowed on the premises?
No. Alcohol, tobacco, and non-vegetarian food are strictly prohibited on our entire premises — including rooms, common areas, and the parking lot. We maintain a fully sattvic environment as a matter of principle.

Facilities & Day Bathing

What is the day bathing facility?
We offer day bathing for pilgrims who don't require an overnight stay. We have 12 clean, well-maintained bathrooms with hot water, available from 5:00 AM. The charge is ₹100 per person.
Is there parking for buses and large vehicles?
Yes, we have a large, free parking area that accommodates cars, SUVs, vans, minibuses, and full-sized tour buses. Parking is free for all guests.
Is WiFi available?
Yes, free WiFi is available across the premises. Please ask at reception for the password.
Do you provide GST invoices for organizations?
Yes, we provide proper GST invoices for religious organizations, trusts, companies, and corporate groups. Please inform us at the time of booking and provide your GST number.

Location & Travel

How far is Cauvery Resorts from Talacauvery?
Cauvery Resorts is approximately 2 km from Talacauvery — the sacred source of River Cauvery. Bhagamandala, the confluence of three rivers, is about 500 m from our resort.
How do we reach Cauvery Resorts?
From Madikeri (40 km via NH-275). From Mangalore (95 km). From Mysore (170 km). From Bangalore (280 km). The nearest airport is Mangalore International Airport. Call us for precise directions or search "Cauvery Resorts Bhagamandala" on Google Maps.
