our story, briefly

Not a five-star. Just a place where someone is expecting you.

We started Cauvery Resorts in 2008 with one simple idea: give pilgrims and families a clean, comfortable place to rest near Talacauvery, so they can stop worrying about everything else.

Since then, monk groups, school groups, religious trusts and families travelling together have stayed with us. Many have come back, and that trust means more to us than any rating.

  • Group stays of 10 to 150 guests, rooms from ₹1,500/night
  • 100% pure vegetarian. No non-veg. No alcohol.
  • Freshen up (6 AM to 7 AM): 12 clean bathrooms with 24-hour hot water at ₹200/person
  • Monk & sanyasi groups welcome, room-only, no meal required
  • Group packages with GST invoicing for trusts
  • Bring your own cook, or we will sort it for your group
  • Free bus, van & car parking

good questions

What people ask us.

Can't find yours? Call +91-944-9485133 and we'll tell you straight.

01What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. For pilgrims needing early darshan at Talacauvery, we offer a 5 AM check-out. Just let us know in advance.

02Is the food 100% vegetarian?

Yes, always. Our kitchen is strictly pure vegetarian and sattvic. No non-veg, no alcohol, ever. We serve South Indian thali meals three times a day. Meals are not included in room rates and are charged separately.

03What is the freshen up facility?

Pilgrims who don't need a room can use our 12 clean bathrooms for just ₹200 per person. No booking needed, walk in anytime.

04Do you accommodate pilgrim and tourist groups?

Of course. Pilgrim and tourist groups are always welcome. We offer room-only stays with no meal obligation, for groups of 10 to 150.

05Can you handle large groups and trusts?

Yes, group stays are all we do. We host groups of 10 to 150 guests. We handle bulk rooms, group meals, bus parking and GST invoicing for religious organisations and trusts.

06Is parking available for tour buses?

Yes, our parking area easily fits tour buses, tempo travellers, vans and cars, free for all guests.
