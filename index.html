Common Questions

01What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. For pilgrims needing early darshan at Talacauvery, we offer a 5 AM check-out — just let us know in advance.

02Is the food 100% vegetarian?

Yes, always. Our kitchen is strictly pure vegetarian and sattvic — no non-veg, no alcohol, ever. We serve South Indian thali meals three times a day.

03What is the Day Bathing facility?

Pilgrims who don't need a room can use our 12 clean bathrooms for just ₹100 per person. No booking needed — walk in anytime.

04Do you accommodate monks and sanyasis?

Of course. Monks and sanyasis are always welcome here. We offer room-only stays with no meal obligation and no minimum stay conditions.

05Can you handle large groups and trusts?

Yes — we've hosted groups of 50 to 500+ guests. We handle bulk rooms, group meals, bus parking, and GST invoicing for religious organizations and trusts.

06Is parking available for tour buses?

Yes, our parking area easily fits tour buses, tempo travellers, vans, and cars — free for all guests.
