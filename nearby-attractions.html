Our resort is perfectly placed to explore the sacred and scenic wonders of Kodagu — all within easy reach.

Sacred & Spiritual Sites

2 km

Talacauvery

The sacred birthplace of River Cauvery — one of the holiest pilgrimage sites in South India. A must-visit for every devotee visiting Coorg.

500 m

Bhagamandala

The Triveni Sangama — confluence of three sacred rivers (Cauvery, Kannike, and the underground Sujyothi). Home to the ancient Bhagandeshwara Temple.

3 km

Brahmagiri Hills

Sacred hilltop offering panoramic views of the Western Ghats and Kerala. A peaceful trekking destination for spiritual seekers and nature lovers alike.

Nature & Scenic Spots

~25 km

Abbey Falls

One of Coorg's most iconic waterfalls, surrounded by coffee and spice plantations. A spectacular sight especially during monsoon season.

~35 km

Raja's Seat, Madikeri

A historic garden and viewpoint where the kings of Coorg once watched the sunset over the misty valleys. Breathtaking sunrise views.

~55 km

Dubare Elephant Camp

Interact with trained elephants at this famous riverside camp on the banks of the Cauvery. Ideal for families and children.
