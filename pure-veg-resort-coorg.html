FAQs

Questions About Our
Pure Veg Resort in Coorg

Is Cauvery Resorts genuinely 100% pure vegetarian?
Yes, completely. No non-vegetarian food has ever been cooked, served, or brought onto our premises. No alcohol is permitted — for guests or staff. This is a non-negotiable policy rooted in our respect for the sacred land of Talacauvery and Bhagamandala.
Do you offer Jain food (no onion/garlic)?
Yes. Sattvic meals without onion and garlic are available on prior request. Please mention this at the time of booking so our kitchen can prepare accordingly.
Can guests bring non-veg food from outside?
No. We politely but firmly request that guests do not bring any non-vegetarian food onto the property. This applies to both cooked food and raw ingredients. Guests who are not comfortable with this policy are advised to look for alternate accommodation.
Are there other pure veg resorts in Coorg?
While a few hotels in Coorg claim to have vegetarian menus, very few are entirely vegetarian — meaning no non-veg food is allowed on the premises at all. Cauvery Resorts is one of the very few fully dedicated pure vegetarian resorts in Coorg.
What is the meal plan at your pure veg resort?
