Kodagu District, Karnataka

Coorg — Where Rivers Begin
and Forests Breathe

ॐ

Nestled in the Western Ghats of Karnataka, Coorg — officially known as Kodagu — is one of India's most celebrated hill destinations. Blanketed in coffee and tea plantations, ancient forests, and mist-covered peaks, this district holds a unique place in the hearts of pilgrims, naturalists, and travellers alike.

Coorg is home to the sacred source of River Cauvery at Talacauvery, the holy Triveni Sangam at Bhagamandala, and the rich biodiversity of Brahmagiri and Nagarhole. It is a land where the divine and the natural exist in extraordinary harmony.

Sacred Places

Spiritual Highlights
of Coorg

Cauvery Resorts is your perfect base for visiting Coorg's most revered pilgrim destinations.

Talacauvery

2 km from resort

The most sacred site in Coorg — the origin point of River Cauvery. A natural spring atop Brahmagiri Hills where millions of devotees come to perform rituals. The Cauvery Sankramana festival draws lakhs of pilgrims annually.

Bhagamandala & Triveni Sangam

500 m from resort

Where the rivers Cauvery, Kannike, and the mythical Sujyothi converge. The Bhagandeshwara temple here is an architectural gem. One of the most spiritually charged sites in Karnataka.

Omkareshwara Temple, Madikeri

~40 km away

A unique temple in Madikeri that blends Islamic and Gothic architectural styles with traditional Hindu temple design. Set beside a serene pond, a testament to Coorg's cultural synthesis.

Natural Wonders

Scenic Attractions
Around Coorg

Coffee Estates

Coorg produces 30% of India's coffee. Walk through lush arabica plantations and learn about the crop that built this region.

Brahmagiri Hills

A scenic trek offering panoramic views of the Western Ghats and the forests below. A popular sunrise trek.

Nagarhole National Park

One of India's premier tiger and elephant reserves — a half-day safari from Coorg, ideal for wildlife lovers.

Iruppu Falls

A sacred waterfall on the banks of Lakshmana Tirtha River — pilgrims and tourists both frequent this majestic site.

Madikeri Fort

Historic Tipu Sultan's Fort in Coorg's capital, with the beautiful Abbey Falls and Raja's Seat viewpoint nearby.

Dubare Elephant Camp

Interact with and bathe elephants at this famous camp on the banks of the Cauvery river near Kushalnagar.

Plan Your Trip

Best Time to
Visit Coorg

Summer (Mar–May)

Pleasantly warm days and cool nights. Ideal for trekking, coffee estate tours, and sightseeing. Less crowded than peak season.

Winter (Oct–Feb) — Best

The ideal season — cool, crisp air, minimal rain, all attractions accessible. Peak season for pilgrims and families. Cauvery Sankramana in October.

Monsoon (Jun–Sep)

Coorg transforms into an emerald paradise. Waterfalls at their peak. Incredibly beautiful but outdoor activities are limited.
