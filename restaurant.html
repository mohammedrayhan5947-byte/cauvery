South Indian Cuisine
Made with Devotion

ॐ

Food, at Cauvery Resorts, is not just a service — it is an act of care. Our in-house restaurant has been serving 100% pure vegetarian South Indian meals since the resort opened in 2008. Every dish is prepared fresh each day using time-honoured recipes and clean, wholesome ingredients.

We maintain a strict no-non-vegetarian policy on our entire premises, making Cauvery Resorts a spiritually safe space for pilgrims, devout Hindu families, and all guests who prefer a sattvic lifestyle.

Flexible cooking arrangements for pilgrim groups: Pilgrims and groups are welcome to bring their own cook or chef — we provide kitchen space and facilities. If your group does not have a cook, we will arrange a cook for you on request. Simply let us know in advance when booking.

Flexible Cooking Arrangements for Pilgrim Groups

We understand that many pilgrimage groups have their own cooking traditions and preferences. At Cauvery Resorts, we warmly accommodate both options:

  Bring Your Own Cook

Your group can bring your own cook or chef. We provide the kitchen space, utensils, gas, and water. Cook as per your group's taste and tradition. Please inform us at the time of booking.

  We Arrange a Cook for You

Don't have a cook? No problem. The resort owner will arrange a cook on your behalf. Simply let us know your group size and meal preferences when booking and we'll take care of the rest.

  To arrange cooking facilities or request a cook, WhatsApp us or call +91-944-9485133 — cooking arrangements apply to all-vegetarian (sattvic) food only.
