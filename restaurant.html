Our Restaurant

Home/Restaurant
nourishing every guest

South Indian food, made with devotion.

Food at Cauvery Resorts is not just a service. It is an act of care. Our in-house restaurant has served 100% pure vegetarian South Indian meals since the resort opened in 2008, and every dish is prepared fresh each day from time-honoured recipes and clean, wholesome ingredients.

There is a strict no-non-vegetarian rule across the whole premises, which makes this a spiritually safe place for pilgrims, devout Hindu families and anyone who prefers a sattvic way of eating.

Cooking for a pilgrim group? You are welcome to bring your own cook, and we will give you the kitchen space and facilities. No cook? We will arrange one on request. Just tell us when you book.

the daily menu

What's on the table.

Fresh, wholesome, home-style South Indian vegetarian meals, served three times a day.

Breakfast

from 6:00 AM

Early service for pilgrims catching first darshan at Talacauvery.

  • Idli with sambar & chutneys
  • Masala dosa / plain dosa
  • Vada & medu vada
  • Upma / pongal
  • Hot filter coffee & tea
  • Fruit juice (seasonal)

Lunch

12:00 PM – 3:00 PM

A traditional South Indian thali, filling and wholesome.

  • South Indian rice thali
  • Sambar rice & rasam rice
  • Curd rice (mosaranna)
  • Dal & mixed vegetable curry
  • Papad & pickle
  • Kodagu special dal

Dinner

7:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Light, sattvic evening meals to end the day peacefully.

  • Chapati / roti with sabzi
  • Rice with dal & curry
  • Curd rice
  • Rasam & papad
  • Seasonal vegetable dishes
  • Payasam / kheer (on request)

for pilgrim groups

Your cook, or ours.

Many pilgrimage groups have their own cooking traditions. We are happy with either way.

Bring your own cook

Your group can bring its own cook or chef. We provide the kitchen space, utensils, gas and water, and you cook to your group's taste and tradition. Please tell us when you book.

or

We arrange a cook

No cook? No problem. The resort owner will arrange one for you. Tell us your group size and meal preferences when you book, and we will take care of the rest.

  To arrange cooking facilities or request a cook, WhatsApp us or call +91-944-9485133.
