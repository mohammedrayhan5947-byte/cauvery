temples at the doorstep

The holy places, close to our gate.

Cauvery Resorts is ideally located as a base for visiting the most sacred sites of the Kodagu pilgrimage circuit. Most of what you came to see is a short walk or drive away.

  1. 5 kmfrom the resort

    Bhagamandala

    The sacred confluence of the rivers Cauvery, Kannike, and the mythical Sujyoti. The Bhagandeshwara temple here is ancient and architecturally magnificent. Pilgrims perform holy dips at the Triveni Sangama. Our resort is just a 5-minute walk from this site.

  2. 2 kmfrom the resort

    Talacauvery

    The sacred source of the River Cauvery, one of the most revered pilgrimage sites in South India. The Brahmagiri peak rises above and the Kotiteertha tank is where pilgrims take a holy dip. The annual Cauvery Theerthodbhava festival draws hundreds of thousands of devotees.

  3. ~35 kmfrom the resort

    Iruppu Falls

    A magnificent waterfall on the Lakshmana Tirtha river, located near the Rameshwara temple. Pilgrims bathe here on Shivaratri. The surrounding Brahmagiri Wildlife Sanctuary makes for a scenic visit.

  4. ~35 kmfrom the resort

    Namdroling Monastery

    The Golden Temple, a stunning Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Bylakuppe with towering golden statues of Buddha. One of the most visited attractions in Coorg, irrespective of faith.

  5. ~40 kmfrom the resort

    Madikeri Fort & Abbey Falls

    Madikeri (Mercara) is the district headquarters of Coorg with the historic Tipu Sultan's Fort, Raja's Seat viewpoint, and the beautiful Abbey Falls nearby.

  6. all aroundthroughout Coorg

    Coorg Coffee Estates

    Coorg is India's premier coffee-growing region. Many estates offer guided walks and farm tours. The misty hillscapes dotted with coffee, pepper, and cardamom are uniquely beautiful.

at Cauvery Resorts

Experiences right here.

Beyond the pilgrimage sites, the resort itself offers moments guests remember long after they return home.

after dark

Campfire with Music

Gather around a crackling campfire with music, songs and stories under the stars. A favourite with college groups, families and pilgrim parties, described as "unforgettable" in our reviews.

Dinner Buffet

For groups, we arrange a dinner buffet with traditional South Indian dishes, a hearty spread after a full day of darshan and sightseeing.

Trekking in the Western Ghats

Guided treks through forest and hill trails, including the climb to Brahmagiri Peak with sweeping views of Coorg. Some of the best trekking in Coorg starts right from our doorstep.

Mountain Top Views

Wake up to panoramic views of the Western Ghats. The Brahmagiri peak and misty valleys are visible from the resort.

Morning Tea on the Jhoola

Sip fresh filter coffee or tea on the swing overlooking the forest, a serene Coorg morning ritual our guests love.

Nature Walks

Stroll through the forest surroundings. The air is clean, the birdlife is rich, and the greenery of the Western Ghats is all around.

Sunrise Prayers

Early check-out at 5 AM for pilgrims who want to attend first darshan at Talacauvery or the morning aarti at Bhagamandala.

Please note: Campfire with music, trekking and other activities are separate paid packages, not included with the rooms. Pricing is discussed on a call, based on your dates and group.
