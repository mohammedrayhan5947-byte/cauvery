our story, briefly

Fifteen years of looking after people.

Cauvery Resorts began in 2008 with a plain, serious purpose: to give the thousands of pilgrims who visit Talacauvery and Bhagamandala every year a clean, comfortable and spiritually appropriate place to rest.

What started as a modest facility has grown, over 15 years, into one of the most trusted names for pilgrim stays in Coorg. Pilgrims, families, monks, religious organisations and spiritual trusts from across South India have slept under this roof.

Our founding idea has not changed: a sattvic environment, genuine hospitality and affordable prices, so that everyone who comes to this sacred land feels at home.

  • Over 10,000 guests served in 15 years
  • Trusted by hundreds of religious organisations

what we stand for

Three promises, written plainly.

We did not write these for a brochure. They are the rules we run the place by.

Spiritual sanctity

We keep a 100% sattvic environment: no non-vegetarian food, no alcohol, no tobacco. It is how we honour the sacred land we sit on.

no exceptions, ever

Genuine hospitality

Every guest, whether pilgrim, family, group or monk, is welcomed warmly and looked after. For us this is not just a business. It is a calling.

pilgrim or family, same welcome

Honest pricing

Affordable rates with no hidden charges. Nobody on a spiritual journey should have to worry about the bill.

meet your host

Our guests do not come here just for a room. They come seeking peace, sacred space, and a home away from home. That is what we try to give every single day.

Mr. Nayak has been at Cauvery Resorts personally since it opened in 2008, welcoming pilgrims, college groups, families, monks and spiritual trusts with the same warmth. Guests on Google Reviews keep describing him as "humble", "courteous" and "genuinely helpful".

His rule is simple: serve every guest as a devotee, not as a customer. That belief, rooted in the meaning of this land, is why so many people trust Cauvery Resorts on the Talacauvery pilgrimage route.
