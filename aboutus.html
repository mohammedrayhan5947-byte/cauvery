Our Story

15+ Years of Serving
Pilgrims & Guests

ॐ

Cauvery Resorts was founded in 2008 with a simple but profound mission: to provide a clean, comfortable, and spiritually appropriate accommodation for the thousands of pilgrims who visit Talacauvery and Bhagamandala every year.

What started as a modest facility has grown, over 15 years, into one of the most trusted names for pilgrim accommodation in Coorg — serving pilgrims, families, monks, religious organizations, and spiritual trusts from across South India.

Our founding principle remains unchanged: a sattvic environment, genuine hospitality, and affordable pricing — so that every devotee who visits this sacred land feels at home.

What We Stand For

Affordable rates with no hidden charges. We believe devotees should not have to worry about cost when on a spiritual journey.
