before you ask

The usual questions.

01Do I need to pay in advance?

No advance payment is needed just to enquire, and none is required for most bookings. A booking is confirmed once we reply with availability.

02What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. For early darshan at Talacauvery we can arrange a 5 AM check-out. Just let us know.

03Do you take small bookings?

We host groups only, from 10 to 150 guests; there are no single-room bookings. Tell us the numbers and we coordinate rooms, meals, bus parking and GST invoicing.
