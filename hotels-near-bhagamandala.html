Bhagamandala is one of Karnataka's most sacred pilgrimage destinations — the divine confluence (Triveni Sangam) of the rivers Cauvery, Kannike, and the unseen Sujyothi. Every year, thousands of devotees come here to take a holy dip, offer prayers at the Bhagandeshwara Temple, and seek blessings.

Cauvery Resorts is right at Bhagamandala — the only dedicated pilgrim resort in the area with clean accommodation, a pure vegetarian restaurant, and group-friendly facilities. We have been serving pilgrims, families, and organizations since 2008.

Sacred Sites

Sacred Places Near Bhagamandala
You Must Visit

Bhagandeshwara Temple

One of Coorg's most revered temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, right at the Triveni Sangam. Ancient Kerala-style architecture.

Triveni Sangam

The confluence of Cauvery, Kannike & Sujyothi. Taking a holy dip here is considered as sacred as bathing at Prayagraj.

Talacauvery (12 km)

The origin point of River Cauvery. Just 12 km away — easily combined into a single day's pilgrimage itinerary.

Brahmagiri Wildlife Sanctuary

A biodiversity hotspot bordering Talacauvery. Spot hornbills, leopards, and rare flora on guided treks.

Iruppu Falls (60 km)

Another sacred waterfall and temple in Coorg. Combine with your Bhagamandala stay for a complete pilgrimage circuit.

Coorg Coffee Estates

The misty Kodagu hills are India's coffee heartland. Walk through an estate and sip authentic Coorg filter coffee.
