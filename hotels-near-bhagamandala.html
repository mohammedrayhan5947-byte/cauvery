Your Hotel at the Heart
of Bhagamandala

Bhagamandala is one of Karnataka's most sacred pilgrimage destinations — the divine confluence (Triveni Sangam) of the rivers Cauvery, Kannike, and the unseen Sujyothi. Every year, thousands of devotees come here to take a holy dip, offer prayers at the Bhagandeshwara Temple, and seek blessings.

Cauvery Resorts is right at Bhagamandala — the only dedicated pilgrim resort in the area with clean accommodation, a pure vegetarian restaurant, and group-friendly facilities. We have been hosting pilgrim groups, family groups, and organizations since 2008.

Questions About Hotels
Near Bhagamandala

01Where is Cauvery Resorts in relation to Bhagamandala?

Cauvery Resorts is located at Bhagamandala, near the Triveni Sangam and Bhagandeshwara Temple. It is the closest dedicated pilgrim resort in the area, making it the most convenient base for your visit.

02Can I do both Bhagamandala and Talacauvery in one trip?

Yes — and this is the most popular itinerary. Talacauvery is just 12 km from Bhagamandala. Most pilgrims do both in a single day, either starting early from Talacauvery and returning to Bhagamandala, or vice versa.

03Is there a hotel near Bhagamandala that serves only vegetarian food?

Yes — Cauvery Resorts is 100% pure vegetarian. We do not serve or permit non-vegetarian food or alcohol on our premises. Our kitchen is sattvic, aligned with the sacred nature of Bhagamandala.
