What We Offer

Everything You Need
for a Comfortable Stay

Every facility at Cauvery Resorts is designed around the needs of pilgrims, families, and groups.

Free WiFi

High-speed internet available across the premises for all guests.

Day Bathing

12 clean bathrooms for day visitors at just ₹100/person. Hot water available.

Free Parking

Large parking area for cars, vans, minibuses, and full-sized tour buses. Free for all guests.

Pure Veg Kitchen

In-house restaurant serving 100% sattvic South Indian vegetarian meals three times daily.

AC Rooms

Air-conditioned deluxe rooms available for guests who prefer premium comfort.

Television

Cable television with remote control available in select room categories.

24/7 Reception

Round-the-clock front desk service. We are always here for our guests.

Hot Water

Hot water in all bathrooms — attached and common — for a comfortable bath any time.

Early Check-Out

Special 5 AM check-out for pilgrims catching morning darshan at Talacauvery.

GST Invoicing

Proper GST invoices for religious organizations, trusts, and corporate groups.

Alcohol-Free Zone

Our entire premises — rooms, restaurant, common areas — is 100% alcohol and non-veg free.

Prime Location

Just 2 km from Talacauvery and 500 m from Bhagamandala — ideal for pilgrims.

for Large Groups

Bulk Room Allocation

We can accommodate groups of 50–500+ guests across dormitories and rooms. Advance booking recommended.

Coordinated Group Meals

Pre-book meals for your entire group. Prasad boxes and packed lunches available for pilgrim processions.

Bus & Vehicle Parking

Large paved parking for tour buses, tempos, and private vehicles. No charge for guests.

GST Invoice for Trusts

Professional GST invoices and receipts for religious trusts and registered organizations.

Dedicated Contact Person

A designated contact person for your group's coordinator throughout your stay.

Day Bathing for Groups

12 bathrooms open from 5 AM. Group rates available for large pilgrim groups.
